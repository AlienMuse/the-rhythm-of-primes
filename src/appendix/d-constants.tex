\section{Constants and exponents}\label{app:constants}
The argument fixes a number of explicit constants and exponents: the moduli of the reduction, the sizes of the data, the exponents of the three main theorems, and the losses of Laplace's method and of the
congruences. The table lists each of them as it is printed in the text, with the file of the Lean development that fixes it. Each entry occurs, as printed, in the text of the paper and, as a Lean expression,
in the named file. Constants that the argument only bounds, such as the constant $C_0$ of \cref{rem:constant-bound}, and the comparison of sizes of
\cref{rem:magnitudes}, are not listed; their role is described where they occur.
