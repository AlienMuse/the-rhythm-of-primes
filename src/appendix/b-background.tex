\section{Background results in the form used}\label{app:background}

This appendix collects the results from the literature on which the argument rests, in the form in
which the Lean development proves and uses them. \Cref{ss:B-analytic-intro} to
\cref{ss:B-zero-symmetry} treat the analytic inputs of \cref{part:reduction}; they lie on the main line
of the argument and are proved in full. The algebraic background of
\cref{part:derivatives,part:upper,part:spanning,part:lower,part:congruences} is stated in the later
sections of this appendix, with its sources.
